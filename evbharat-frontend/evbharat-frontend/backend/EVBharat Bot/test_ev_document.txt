Electric Vehicle Policy Test Document
==========================================

FAME II Scheme Details:
- Total budget: ₹10,000 crores over 3 years
- Support for 10 lakh electric two-wheelers
- Support for 5 lakh electric three-wheelers  
- Support for 55,000 electric four-wheelers
- Support for 7,090 electric buses

State Incentives:
Maharashtra: Additional ₹5,000 subsidy on electric two-wheelers
Gujarat: 100% road tax exemption for EVs
Karnataka: 15% capital subsidy for EV manufacturing

Charging Infrastructure:
- Target: 2,877 public charging stations
- Fast charging stations every 25 km on highways
- Normal charging stations every 3 km in cities

Battery Requirements:
- Minimum energy density: 80 Wh/kg
- Warranty: 3 years or 75,000 km
- Made in India certification required for incentives
